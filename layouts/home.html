SideScopes Lab

Change an image and watch the scopes respond

The Interactive Lab runs the application’s scope engines in your browser. Start with a supplied image or load your own, select a region, and adjust color and tone while the traces update.

The adjustment controls are designed for observation and teaching; they do not reproduce the processing of any particular editor. Images you load remain in your browser.

Open SideScopes Lab

Desktop and Lab

The Lab analyzes still images inside its page. The desktop application measures other applications, follows windows and faces, and keeps its region visible on the desktop.

Measurement boundary

Display-referred, SDR measurements

SideScopes measures pixels after the source application and operating system have rendered them to the screen. It does not inspect source media, timeline values, RAW data, or exported files, and it is not an ICC soft-proofing or HDR signal-analysis tool.

This boundary matters when interpreting clipping, gamut, white balance, and color-difference readings. The learning pages state the relevant conditions alongside each reading.

What SideScopes measures

Open source and local

SideScopes has no account, telemetry, updater, or network access. Its source and build history are public, and release downloads are served by GitHub.

The project is under active development before version 1.0. macOS and Windows builds are available; Linux is not currently released.
