## What this is, and what it is not

The scopes here are the application's own: the same C++ engines, drawn by
the same code, so a trace you learn to read here is the trace you will get
after downloading. The toolbar, the status readout, the right-click menu and
the keyboard are the application's too.

What is missing is the part a browser cannot do. **No page can read another
program's window**, so there is nothing to point the scopes at except a
picture you give them — which is why the region here sits on a photograph
rather than on your screen. Attaching a region to a window, following a
face, and the desktop border all belong to the installed application and
cannot be demonstrated.

## Your picture stays here

The photograph is decoded by your browser and handed straight to the
analysis. It is not uploaded, and there is no code on the page that could
upload it.
